Egypt: A Land of Ancient History

Egypt is a country in northeastern Africa, famous for its ancient civilization, remarkable monuments and rich cultural heritage. The River Nile flows through the country and has supported communities for thousands of years.

Visitors from around the world come to see the pyramids, temples, museums and historic cities. Ancient Egyptian achievements in architecture, art, writing and engineering continue to interest people today.

Places to explore

  • The Great Pyramids of Giza
  • The Great Sphinx
  • Luxor and Karnak Temples
  • The River Nile
